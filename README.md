## Overview

**Logistics Control Tower Agent** is a reference AI workflow for Transportation & Logistics organizations. It addresses a practical operations challenge:

> Operations teams need to re-plan orders, vehicles, warehouses, traffic and customer ETAs when conditions change.

The workflow is triggered when **Disruption or control-tower signal**. It coordinates Disruption, route and customer communication agents and keeps people responsible for decisions that require judgment, exception handling, or a sensitive business action.

## Why This AI Workflow Matters

Organizations often have the data and systems needed to solve this problem, but the information is spread across teams and operational tools. An AI agent workflow can help bring the right context together, explain the next step, and carry out approved actions without hiding the decision trail.

This is an architecture pattern, not a promise of automatic results. Production implementation depends on data quality, system access, workflow evaluation, security controls, and a clear human review design.

## How It Works

1. **Detect disruption**
2. **Evaluate alternative routes and resources**
3. **Re-plan delivery**
4. **Request operational approval**
5. **Update customer ETA and monitor**

## AI Agents Involved

Disruption, route and customer communication agents.

## Human Oversight and Security Controls

**Human checkpoint:** Approve material route, cost and service-level trade-offs.

Recommended controls for this AI agent use case:

- Dispatcher identity
- Route boundaries
- Approval policy
- ETA evidence
- Audit trail

These controls define what the AI system can see, recommend, or do, and when a person must review the outcome. High-impact actions should fail closed when identity, policy, or approval information is missing.
